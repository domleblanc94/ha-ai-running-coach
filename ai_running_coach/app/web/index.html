    PLAN YOUR RACE

    Understand the course. Choose your approach.

    Upload a running or trail-race GPX, then work with your coach on two strategies using your saved profile and activity history.

    Importing a course uses no AI request. Generate or refine uses at most one AI request from the same daily allowance as training reviews. An identical completed request can reuse its saved result.

    Missing heights are looked up automatically by sending sampled coordinates to Open-Meteo. OpenStreetMap receives the map area, your IP and Home Assistant site address, without access tokens. Your athlete profile and GPX file are not uploaded to these services.

    Map privacy and elevation service terms

    Open-Meteo supplies estimated Copernicus surface heights. Its free service is for personal, non-commercial use and may retain request coordinates for up to 90 days. Files with complete heights use their GPX data without an elevation lookup. Map tiles use normal browser caching; you can hide the map below.
